About me

I got into photography chasing light. I stayed for the stories.

Hi, I'm Deginet Desta Mena (Degi) — the photographer and videographer behind Degi Pictures. What started as covering friends' weddings on weekends has grown into a full studio practice spanning weddings, graduations, ceremonies, portraits and aerial work across Ethiopia and beyond.

My approach is simple: show up early, stay observant, and let the day unfold naturally in front of the camera. I edit every set myself, frame by frame, so the final gallery feels consistent, warm and true to how the day actually felt.

When I'm not shooting, I'm usually testing new gear, flying the drone somewhere new, or going back through old albums looking for the shot I almost missed.
